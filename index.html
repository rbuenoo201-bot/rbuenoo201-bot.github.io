Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ROA Bueno Estates ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our real estate acquisition outreach, including SMS text message communications.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Name and contact information (phone number, mailing address)
  • Property address and ownership information obtained from public records
  • Communications you send to us via SMS or other channels

2. How We Obtain Your Information

Contact information is obtained through publicly available sources, including county property records, tax assessor databases, and other public real estate records. We do not purchase consumer marketing lists.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Contact property owners regarding potential real estate transactions
  • Respond to inquiries you initiate with us
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use your information for any purpose unrelated to real estate acquisition, and we do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third-party marketers.

4. SMS Text Message Communications

We may contact you via SMS to inquire about your property. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

To opt out at any time, reply STOP to any message you receive from us. Once you reply STOP, you will receive no further SMS messages from our number. You may also reply HELP for assistance.

By continuing to engage in SMS conversation with us, you acknowledge receipt of this policy and agree to receive SMS messages related to your property.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Property owner information for contacts who have opted out is retained solely to honor that opt-out request.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To submit a request, contact us at the information below.
